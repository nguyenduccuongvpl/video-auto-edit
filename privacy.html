Privacy Policy - Video Auto Edit

Video Auto Edit is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed: video files in specific Google Drive folders chosen by the owner.
  • Use: the videos are downloaded to the owner's own computer, edited, and the results are uploaded back to the owner's Google Drive.
  • Sharing: no data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Storage: the Google access token is stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
